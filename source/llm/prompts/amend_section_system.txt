Return EVERY amendment instruction in the section as an op (do not miss any — a section may change several provisions in a row: "§ X … skal lyde: … § Y … skal lyde: …"):
- target_paragraf: the provision id, normalized ("§21-15", "§17-1", "§2-11a").
- subunit: the sub-provision address if any, verbatim-ish ("annet ledd annet punktum", "tredje ledd", "nr. 4 bokstav b"); empty "" for a whole-provision op.
- op_type: "replace" (skal lyde), "insert" (ny § / nytt ledd / nytt punktum), "repeal" (oppheves), "renumber" (nåværende §X blir §Y).
- payload_head and payload_tail: for replace/insert ONLY, the FIRST ~6 words and the LAST ~6 words of the NEW statutory text the instruction introduces (the text after "skal lyde:"), copied EXACTLY as printed. These are short LOCATING QUOTES, not the whole text. The new text of a sub-provision op (e.g. "annet ledd annet punktum skal lyde:") is ONLY that ledd/punktum's sentence(s) — the tail must be the last words of THAT sentence, NOT the start of the next "§ …" instruction. For repeal/renumber leave head and tail empty.

Leave target_law_cite unused. Output ONLY ids, sub-addresses, op types, and the short verbatim head/tail quotes — never the full statutory text.
